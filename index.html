about me ♡

hi! i'm nova. i’m a highschool artist who spends most of my free time drawing, gaming, and collecting things that make me happy. i play games like baldur’s gate 3 and avatar: frontiers of pandora, and i listen to mostly kpop, indie, and riot grrrl inspired music. a lot of my money goes into concerts, albums, photocards, and merch for the artists and characters i love. i really enjoy expressing my interests in an oshikatsu-like way through decorating my room, my bags, my outfits, and the things around me with fandoms and characters that mean a lot to me.
